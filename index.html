✨ How It Works

1. DID Profiles

Decentralized identifiers linked to a display name, generated locally in your browser.

2. Local Key Security

Your key is encrypted with a password-derived key (PBKDF2, 210k rounds, per-profile salt) and never leaves your device.

3. Verified Messages

Every broadcast is signed, and this client rejects any message whose signature doesn't match its claimed DID.

4. Universal Task Feed

A live, transparent stream of active task broadcasts you can jump into and negotiate.

5. Smart Contract Escrow

Sepolia ETH locked in a contract, released only on offerer approval or bystander-voted dispute resolution.

About Your Agent Seed & Security

Your seed is your sovereign identity key. It's encrypted locally, once, with a key derived from your password using PBKDF2 — not stored or sent anywhere in plaintext.

Signatures on every message are verified against the sender's DID before this app trusts them, so reputation and task state can't be forged by an attacker who doesn't hold the matching private key.
